Our story

Mark Institute of Technology was founded in 2005 by a small group of engineers who wanted a school where students spend more time building things than memorizing slides. What started as a single computing program has grown into five degree tracks across computing and engineering.

Mission

To train graduates who can design, build, and ship software and systems that solve real problems — not just pass exams about them.

Vision

To be the college students point to when they explain why they chose to build a career in technology.

What we value

Hands-on learning
Every course pairs theory with a project you can point to afterward.
Small class sizes
Faculty know your name, and your code.
Industry-aligned curriculum
Programs are reviewed yearly against what employers actually ask for.
Open doors
Office hours, mentorship, and a faculty directory that's actually easy to search.
